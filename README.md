## About me

I'm a mobile-focused Full Stack Developer from Manaus, Brazil, with 7+ years building digital products with **React Native, Flutter and Kotlin Multiplatform**, backed by **Node.js/NestJS** services and **PostgreSQL/PostGIS** databases.

Today I work on digital health innovation with **CITS Albert Einstein**, shipping apps that reach pregnant women, athletes and public-health managers in Brazil's national health system (SUS). Before that, I built e-commerce platforms for 4 European countries (while living in Portugal) and led hospital system rollouts in the Amazon. With 18 years in technology, from IT support to architecture, I like to see the whole picture: from screen to server, from user to business.

In my spare time I like to play games and look for some waves in the surf. 

## What I'm working on

| Project | What it is | Stack |
|---|---|---|
| **Vital Mommy** | Pregnancy tracking app (iOS and Android) with week-by-week content validated by Einstein specialists, gamification (XP, badges, missions, quizzes), 3 languages (PT/EN/ES) and offline-first architecture | React Native, Expo, TypeScript, Jest |
| **TRADA Sport** | Health and sports telemetry platform (Android + Web): onboarding, OTP/PIN authentication, shared UI with Compose Multiplatform, Docker Compose environment | Kotlin Multiplatform, Jetpack Compose, Ktor, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Docker, AWS |
| **VERACIS (PROADI-SUS)** | Platform linking climate and environmental data to the health of vulnerable communities, including quilombola communities, across 6 Brazilian biomes, with interactive maps | React, Next.js, NestJS, PostGIS, MapBox, Leaflet, ArcGIS |
